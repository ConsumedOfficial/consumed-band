01 / OUR STORY

A new band.
A loud beginning.

Consumed began with Eric, Annie, and Nathan — and a shared drive to make music together.

Founded in 2025–2026, the band brings together five young, up-and-coming artists finding their voice in rock and metal. With guitars, bass, drums, and shared vocals, they are building something of their own.

Founded by Eric Gower, Annie Surtees & Nathan Morgan.
