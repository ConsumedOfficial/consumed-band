01 / Our Story

A New Band.
A Loud Beginning.

Consumed began with Eric, Annie and Nathan — and a shared drive to make music together.

Founded in 2025–2026, the band brings together five young, up-and-coming artists finding their voice in rock and metal.

With guitars, bass, drums and shared vocals, Consumed are building something of their own.

Founded by Eric Gower, Annie Surtees & Nathan Morgan.
